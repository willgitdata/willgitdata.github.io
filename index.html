Data Scientist / AI Engineer, Apr 2023 to present  ·  Data Scientist 1, May 2021 to Apr 2023

llm agents healthcare evals in ci

An LLM agent that recommends which programs and services would most help a person in need

Problem

Finding the right social care programs for a person was manual: searching programs, checking eligibility, and deciding referrals by hand. That made navigation slow and inconsistent.

What I built

  • A next-best-action LLM agent for social care referrals
  • Tools for program and resource search, eligibility checks, and referral history
  • Part of the multi-agent LLM systems I build on EHR and social determinants of health data

How I evaluated it

  • Eval gates in CI/CD, so quality regressions block a deploy
  • Accuracy scored against expert review
