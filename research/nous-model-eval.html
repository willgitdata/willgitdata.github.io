05 / THE FINE PRINT

Explore the results. Keep the limits.

A small, task-specific snapshot — not a universal model ranking.

How are answers scored?

Runnable connection-pool, parser and SQL answers are scored by local hidden tests: pass is 10, fail is 0. Other answers use the existing single-judge ratings. Quality is the mean over the common task set. The recorded scores are unchanged in this redesign.

What did the models actually do?

They answered through Odysseus’s Compare chat mode. The models did not edit files or run tests inside their panes. The evaluator executed supported answers afterward. This is not a full agent-loop benchmark.

What do the prices include?

Provider-reported billed costs, including hidden reasoning. Figures sum the retained result for each task; they are not an account-balance ledger and do not include every debugging or retry request. Provider prompt formatting and tokenization can differ.

How does MQD work?

MQD = (model score − baseline score) ÷ (model cost − baseline cost). It is shown only for a more expensive model with a price gap above 25% of the baseline cost. The default baseline is the cheapest fully covered model at or above the field’s median score. This is an exploratory slope, not a validated new benchmark.

How certain is the ranking?

Single recorded samples and a single judge. Some early answers were anonymized, but model identities were visible during later scoring; this is not a fully blind study. No repeat-run variance or inter-rater agreement was measured. Tiny score gaps are not evidence of a reliable capability difference.

Which models are included?

The original selected Nous API roster plus Claude Haiku 5.5 and Gemini 4 Argon. This is a fixed historical selection, not a claim to be today’s newest ten models. Only models with full common-task coverage enter the leaderboard. Missing answers remain missing, not zero-quality results.
